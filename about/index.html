BSS Optimizer

About

BSS Optimizer is a free fan project that makes Bee Swarm Simulator decisions easier. It has four tools: a blue hive optimizer, a Supreme Star Amulet rater, a ticket calculator, and a planter special drops tracker.

Where the advice comes from

The hive and amulet ratings reflect the creator’s own judgment, built from community guides and from watching experienced players answer questions. They are opinions, not official numbers, and the game can change. Ticket prices and planter drop data come from community wiki information and player testing and may be incomplete or out of date.
