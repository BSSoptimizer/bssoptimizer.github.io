BSS Optimizer

About

BSS Optimizer is a free fan project that makes Bee Swarm Simulator decisions easier. It currently has a blue hive optimizer and a Supreme Star Amulet rater.

Where the advice comes from

The ratings and recommendations reflect the creator’s own judgment, built from community guides and from watching experienced players answer questions. They are opinions, not official numbers, and the game can change.
