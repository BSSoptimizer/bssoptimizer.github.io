How the rating works

A Supreme Star Amulet always has a x2.5 Capacity bonus, one or two passives, and exactly five of nine possible stats. This tool scores that combination for a blue hive and gives you three results.

  • Desired stats: how many of the five stats that matter for a blue hive the amulet has: Blue Pollen, Convert Rate, Bee Ability Rate, Pollen, and Critical Chance.
  • Stat score: how strong those rolls are. Blue Pollen and Convert Rate count the most.
  • Blue hive score: an overall score that adds the passives to the stat score.

Passives for a blue hive

Pop Star is required for a blue hive, so an amulet without it gets no rating. Guiding Star and Star Shower only help when paired with Pop Star, and Star Shower is the stronger of the two. Gummy Star, Scorching Star, and Star Saw do not help a blue hive, but they do not hurt either.

Good to know

The scoring is based on the creator’s judgment and community experience, not official game data. Looking for more help with your hive? Try the Blue Hive Optimizer.
