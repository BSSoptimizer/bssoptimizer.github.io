How the calculator works

Enter how many tickets you have and the price of your next ticket from the ticket shop. Then pick the items you want to buy, or type how many tickets you need. The calculator works out how many more tickets you need, then adds up the rising ticket shop prices to give you the honey cost.

Good to know

The shop only shows a rounded price, so the honey cost is an estimate. The prices come from community data and may change if the game changes. Looking for more help? Try the Blue Hive Optimizer or the Supreme Star Amulet Rater.
