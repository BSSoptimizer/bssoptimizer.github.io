About these tools

These are free, fan-made tools for Bee Swarm Simulator players. The hive, bee, amulet, and ticket details you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to us. The planter tracker saves your choices in your browser’s local storage. Analytics and ads are separate, so see the Privacy Policy for details. Read more about the project.
