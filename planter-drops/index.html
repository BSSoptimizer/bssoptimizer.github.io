How it works

Some planters give a guaranteed special drop when you harvest them fully grown in a certain field, then the drop goes on cooldown. Select the planters you own and this tool lists every special drop you could collect, grouped by material. Mark a drop as collected and it is crossed off with a countdown until you can earn it again. Some drops need a chain: harvest the same planter in each listed field in order.

Your planters and timers are saved in this browser only, so they stay on your device. Clearing your browser data will erase them.

Good to know

Drops and cooldowns come from the community wiki and player testing and may be incomplete or out of date, and drops with an unknown cooldown are left out. Drops with no cooldown are always shown as ready. Planters grow in real playtime, and you can only have 3 planters active at once. Looking for more help with your hive? Try the Blue Hive Optimizer.
