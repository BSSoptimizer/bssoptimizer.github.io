Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

BSS Optimizer (“we”, “us”) is a free, unofficial fan website for Bee Swarm Simulator players, run by an independent individual. You can reach us through the contact page.

What you enter

The tools run in your browser. The hive, bee, amulet, and ticket details you enter are not sent to our servers, and we do not collect them.

Local storage

The planter drops tracker saves the planters you select and your collection timers in your browser’s local storage so they are still there when you return. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser’s site data.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, browser, device, and pages viewed. We do not use it to identify you. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Advertising and consent messages

We use Google AdSense features, including Google’s Offerwall. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, and may show you consent or choice messages, including an Offerwall that lets you choose how to continue to a page, for example by viewing an ad. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can manage personalized ads at adssettings.google.com or opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at optout.aboutads.info.

Other services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may receive your IP address and basic browser information when you load a page.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may be asked for consent to cookies and ads, and you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. Residents of some US states have similar rights, including the right to opt out of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information ourselves. Because ads and analytics are provided by Google, the controls described above are the main way to manage that data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
